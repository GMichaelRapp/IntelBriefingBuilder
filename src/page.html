How to write the body

Charts, tables, timelines, key numbers, and images. Give each one a short name, then place it in the body or an appendix with [[name]] on a line of its own. Figures and tables are numbered in the order they appear.

Each # heading starts Appendix A, B, C. ## headings are numbered A.1, A.2. [[rating-key]] adds the source rating key table.

After the last chapter: acknowledgments, appendices, a glossary, a note on sources. Each # heading starts a new page and is listed in the contents, unnumbered. Notes, citations, and visuals work here as in the chapters. [[rating-key]] adds the source rating key table.

Cite sources from your OSINT Citation Generator lists. In the generator, save your list to a file; open that file here. Each source gets a short key.

Write [@key] or [@key, p. 45] in the text: the first citation of a source prints in full in a footnote, later ones in short form.
